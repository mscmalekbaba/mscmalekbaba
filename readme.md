# Hi, I'm Malek Baba 👋

I'm a Software Engineer based in Germany, with a master's degree in Computer Science from the University of Stuttgart.

Alongside full-stack and agentic systems, I do research on **efficient video understanding for industrial settings**: making models small and fast enough to recognise what people do on real production lines, and now exploring how vision-language models fit in.

## Engineering

I enjoy architecting and building full-stack web applications, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems, and agentic workflows, from concept to evaluation:
[Multi-agent root-cause analysis](https://github.com/mscmalekbaba/RCA-Analysis-Via-Multi-Agents) · [Sports Brief Builder](https://github.com/mscmalekbaba/Agentic-Sport-Briefs) · [DocTongue](https://github.com/mscmalekbaba/DocTongue)

## Research

- **Published:** [PDNet: Exploring Pruning and Distillation for Real-Time Worker Activity Recognition in Industrial Assembly](https://github.com/mscmalekbaba/PDNet), first author, VISAPP 2026 (DOI: 10.5220/0014477200004084). Structured pruning plus knowledge distillation on a 3D-CNN (X3D-M), evaluated on MECCANO.
- **Master's thesis:** [Resource-efficient Deep Learning for Real-time Recognition of Worker Activities During Industrial Assembly](https://github.com/mscmalekbaba/Master-Thesis-Resource-Efficient-PySlowFast), built on PySlowFast.
- **In progress:** an exploratory study of open VLMs (Qwen-VL family) on MECCANO, compared with PDNet on accuracy and compute cost. *(link coming soon)*

### Research interests
- Video understanding with VLMs: action recognition, temporal grounding, long-video reasoning
- Robustness with little labelled data: new stations, viewpoints, occlusions
- Model compression and distillation for real-time deployment
